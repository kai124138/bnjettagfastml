# Particle Transformer for Jet Tagging

- **Authors:** Huilin Qu, Congqiao Li, Sitian Qian
- **Venue / Year:** ICML 2022 (Proceedings of the 39th International Conference on Machine Learning); 2022
- **arXiv:** 2202.03772 — https://arxiv.org/abs/2202.03772
- **PDF:** https://arxiv.org/pdf/2202.03772
- **Code:** https://github.com/jet-universe/particle_transformer
- **Topic area:** Jet tagging & transformers

## TL;DR
Introduces **JetClass**, a 100M-jet public dataset (~2 orders of magnitude larger than prior public sets, 10 jet types), and **Particle Transformer (ParT)**, a transformer that injects pairwise particle-interaction features into the attention mechanism. ParT surpasses the previous state-of-the-art ParticleNet by a large margin and is now a standard high-accuracy baseline for jet tagging.

## Summary
Jet tagging is a critical but difficult classification task in particle physics. Deep learning has greatly improved performance, but progress was bottlenecked by the lack of a large-scale public dataset. The authors release JetClass — 100M simulated jets spanning 10 jet categories, including types not previously explored for tagging. On this dataset they propose Particle Transformer (ParT), a transformer-based architecture that augments self-attention with pairwise particle interaction terms (a learned attention bias derived from physically motivated particle-pair features). ParT outperforms a plain transformer and exceeds ParticleNet, and the authors show that pre-training ParT on JetClass and fine-tuning transfers strong performance to smaller benchmark datasets.

## Key methods / contributions
- **JetClass dataset**: 100M jets, 10 classes (incl. H→bb/cc/gg/4q/lvqq, t→bqq', t→blv, W→qq, Z→qq, q/g), released publicly to enable large-scale training.
- **Particle Transformer (ParT)**: transformer over jet-constituent inputs with a **particle interaction matrix** added as an attention bias (encodes pairwise kinematic features such as Δ, kT, z, m²).
- Demonstrates **transfer learning**: pre-train on JetClass, fine-tune on top tagging (Top Quark Tagging Reference) and quark/gluon benchmarks for further gains.
- Establishes ParT as the new SOTA over ParticleNet across multiple benchmarks.
- Provides full open-source training/inference code (Weaver framework) and pre-trained weights.

## Main results
ParT surpasses the previous state-of-the-art ParticleNet by a large margin on JetClass, and a plain transformer baseline as well; the interaction-augmented attention is the key driver (confirmed qualitatively in search results). Reports state-of-the-art accuracy/AUC/background-rejection on the standard top-tagging and quark/gluon benchmarks after fine-tuning (specific numeric AUC/rejection values: see paper).

## Relevance to BNJetTag
ParT is **the canonical transformer-over-particle-cloud jet tagger** and the direct architectural lineage for BNJetTag — our BitNet model is a small transformer over jet-constituent ("particle cloud") inputs, i.e. a quantized descendant of this family. JetClass is the natural large-scale benchmark, and ParT/ParticleNet full-precision AUCs are the accuracy targets our 1-bit {−1,+1}-weight model aims to approach. The interaction-attention idea is also relevant: if we adopt a pairwise-feature attention bias, we must consider how it survives binarization. ParT defines "full precision" for the AUC-vs-resource trade-off central to our thesis.
